# We need a hub

Inter gets a Substack on a custom domain, such as blog.inter.one.

- Taylor and Sandeep are the authors
- Substack over a self-hosted blog, to move fast
- Exporting later is possible
- Personal accounts aren't company channels, but they drive eyeballs

---

<!-- .slide: class="split" -->

# Distribution

No company social accounts yet. Inter's LinkedIn or X would start with zero followers.

- Taylor's and Sandeep's personal accounts do the distribution for now
- The goal: subscriptions to the Substack

---

# Promotion is half the work.

Each weekly longform post gets a 300–500 word hook and pull-quote snippets, ready for X, LinkedIn and Substack Notes.

---



> what's Inter?

Posting early doesn't steal the moment; we get to double dip. The first posts say who we are and what the blog covers, so readers arriving after the announcements find something already there.
